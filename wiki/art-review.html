Fourteen distinct compositions, derived from the same rendered models. Semantic emblems distinguish discovery, fabrication, containment, efficiency, and productivity at inventory scale.

At a glance

Built to ship. Kept editable.

Body sprites, isolated emission, and directional ground shadows share a fixed render origin. Icons and technology art are derived from the model heroes. Source scenes, sample boards, and file checksums are included for future iteration.
